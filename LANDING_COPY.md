## FAQ (for landing page)

**Is my data safe?**
No login required. Your tools and spend data are stored anonymously. The shareable URL strips your email.

**Do I need to pay?**
Always free. SpendWiseAI is free forever. We make money only if you choose to work with Credex.

**How accurate are the recommendations?**
All pricing verified weekly from official pricing pages (see PRICING_DATA.md). Rules are based on plan feature differences, not guesses.

**What is Credex?**
Credex negotiates volume discounts on AI API credits for startups. They reach out only for audits with >$500/mo in savings.

## Copy Rationale

- "Stop Burning Cash" outperforms "Optimise Your AI Spend" — loss aversion > gain framing (ref: user interview 1: "lunch for the whole team")
- "2 minutes" sets expectation and reduces form abandonment
- Avoiding the word "free" in the CTA button — "Start Free Audit" > "Get Free Report" (sounds more like a tool, less like a lead magnet)
