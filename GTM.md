# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Target User (Specific, Not Vague)

**Primary persona:** CTO or Founder at a 3–25 person B2B SaaS startup paying $500–5,000/month on AI tools.

- Uses 4–7 AI subscriptions simultaneously
- Approves invoices without auditing utilization because "it's small relative to AWS"
- Assumes "team plan = right choice" by default
- Has at least one "I meant to cancel that" tool they've been paying for 3+ months

**Evidence from user interviews:** A.K. (CTO, 6-person) said "I just approve invoices without checking. It's a rounding error compared to AWS." That's the unlock — they don't audit because they assume waste is small. But it isn't.

## First 100 Users in 30 Days ($0 Budget)

**Week 1:**

1. **Post to Hacker News (Day 1, Tuesday 9am EST):** "Show HN: Free AI spend auditor — found $840/mo in waste in my own stack"
   - HN upvotes = 100–300 visitors/day for 48 hours
   - Target: 50 audits week 1

2. **Twitter thread (Day 2):** "I audited 10 startup AI stacks. Here's what I found." with anonymized screenshots.
   - Tag @levelsio, @shl, @steventey (they amplify cost-related content)
   - Target: 30 audits

3. **DM 50 founders on LinkedIn (Day 3–5):**
   - Search filter: CTO, 10–50 employees, mentions "AI" in profile
   - Message: "Hey [name], I built a free tool that audits AI spend. No signup. Takes 2 min. Here's the link."
   - Target: 15 audits from DMs

**Week 2–4:**

4. **Product Hunt launch (Day 8, Tuesday 12:01am PST):** Schedule in advance. PH audience is exactly the target.
5. **Post to 5 Slack communities:** Indie Hackers, MicroConf, AI Engineer Discord, The Product Folks, Buildspace alumni.
6. **Referral loop:** Add "Share your savings" button on result page. Twitter share = +1 audit for free.

**Total by Day 30:** ~200 audits, 80 leads, 3–5 shareable URLs circulating.

## The Unfair Distribution Channel

Credex's existing network of **200+ portfolio founders** who already trust the brand.

One email from Credex to their portfolio: "We built a free tool to audit your AI spend. It's not a sales pitch — it's genuinely useful. Here's the link."

That single email = 50+ audits on day 1. No other AI spend auditor has that warm intro to 200 decision-makers.

## Pivot Signals

| If this happens... | Then pivot by... |
|--------------------|------------------|
| Share rate <5% after 100 audits | Redesign result page savings headline — it's not compelling enough to share |
| Lead capture rate <40% | Remove "company name" field (friction) and A/B test |
| High-savings audits <15% | Tighten rule engine — too many false positives diluting signal |
| Consultation click rate <8% | A/B test CTA copy ("See how much more you could save" vs "Book consultation") |
