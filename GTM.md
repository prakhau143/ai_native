# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Target User

**Primary:** CTO or Founder of a 3–25 person startup paying $300–$5,000/month on AI tools.
- Uses 3–6 AI subscriptions simultaneously
- Approves invoices without auditing utilisation
- Thinks "team plan = right choice" by default

**Secondary:** Finance/ops lead at 25–100 person company noticing growing AI line items.

## Where They Are

| Channel | Specific community | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Hacker News | Show HN post (Tuesday 9am EST) | Founders who care about cost/infra; Show HN posts with real savings numbers perform well |
| Reddit | r/SaaS, r/entrepreneur, r/startups, r/MachineLearning | Active discussions about AI tool costs; post the audit link in relevant threads |
| Indie Hackers | #show-iH channel, monthly "what are you paying for?" threads | Product-aware builders who discuss spend openly |
| Twitter/X | #buildinpublic, #SaaS, Lenny Rachitsky's followers | Viral sharing of result screenshots ("I saved $840/mo!") |
| Discord | Buildspace alumni server (#general), AI Engineer Discord, The Product Folks | High-trust async communities of builders paying for AI tools |
| Slack | Indie Hackers Slack (#deals), MicroConf community Slack | Paid communities with engaged founders |
| LinkedIn | CTO/VP Eng at 10-50 person SaaS startups (search filter) | Decision-makers who approve AI tool invoices |
| Newsletters | TLDR AI (sponsor or newsletter swap), The Rundown AI, Ben's Bites | Already paying subscribers for AI products = pre-qualified leads |

## Week-1 Traction Plan

1. **Day 1:** Post "Show HN: Free AI spend auditor — found $840/mo in savings in my own stack"
2. **Day 2:** Tweet thread: "I audited 10 startup AI stacks. Here's what I found." (use anonymised real data)
3. **Day 3:** DM 50 founders on LinkedIn who mention AI tools in their profiles
4. **Day 5:** Submit to Product Hunt (schedule for 12:01 AM PST Tuesday)

**Target week 1:** 200 audits, 40 leads, 5 shareable result URLs circulating.

## Viral Loop

Result page has "I saved $X/mo on AI tools" social share button → OG image auto-generates → Twitter/LinkedIn click-throughs → new audits.

## Unfair Distribution Channel

Credex's existing network of 200+ portfolio founders who already trust the brand. One email from Credex = 50+ audits day 1.

## Week-4 Pivot Signal

If share rate < 5% after 200 audits, the result page isn't compelling enough — redesign the savings headline. If lead-to-consultation rate < 8%, the high-savings CTA copy needs work.
